THE FOUNDRY™

2025

currents: typesetting

research, typography, web, satire, licensing systems

visit site ↗

THE FOUNDRY™ started with the question: who gets to use t y p e — and who built that system?

Eight weeks of research into licensing, authorship, control, and the weird little laws that decide who gets access to form. Then eight weeks making a tool out of that frustration. Somewhere between a font foundry, a paywall, a joke, and a bureaucratic trap.

It is deliberately overbuilt because licensing is overbuilt. FDRY turns type into something monitored, negotiated, surrendered to. Digital tools love to say they democratize design, but sometimes they just move the locked door from money to technical literacy.

This project makes you click through the door until the door starts sounding ridiculous.

language

FDRY stands for Foundry, Dominion, Registry, and Yield: a fake institutional vocabulary for a very real feeling of permission being withheld.
