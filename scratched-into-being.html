SCRATCHED INTO BEING

2025

nyc / the invisible

photography, essay, book design, web, three.js

visit site ↗

Scratched Into Being documents the desperate small marks left by people trying to prove they exist in a city designed to make them disappear.

These are not just acts of vandalism. They are tiny rebellions. Proof that someone was here. Someone mattered. Someone pressed hard enough to leave a mark.

The project became a physical book with an acrylic cover, mixed vellum paper, hand-sewn binding, and an interactive website where drawing sits on top of archived images.

It is about not wanting to be forgotten, and the strange tenderness of evidence.
