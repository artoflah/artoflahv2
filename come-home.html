Come Home is a riso-printed publication about how home is not always a place. Sometimes it is a person.

The book moves through letters my mom wrote during my freshman year and text messages from across our relationship. Her voice and mine keep touching, blurring, interrupting each other.

Printed in warm pink and orange tones on vellum paper, the book feels like a scrapbook, a receipt, a soft argument, and a way of saying I miss you without making it too neat.
