FOREVER YOURS, LADYBUG

2025

for my boyfriend

book design, cyanotype, coptic binding, motion graphic

Forever Yours, Ladybug is a devotional archive about being afraid of being loved.

Federal blue cotton duck canvas on the outside. Coptic stitched. Cyanotyped fabric pages and glass. I like processes that take too long.

Somewhere between a diary, a prayer book, and a receipt for every time my boyfriend tried to get close and I flinched.

You can open it. Or just hold it. Either one is kind of the point.
