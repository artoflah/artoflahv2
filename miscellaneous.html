Ya’aburnee

translation & motion project

2024

Ya’aburnee is an Arabic expression meaning “you bury me.” It describes the wish that someone you love will outlive you because life without them would be unbearable. The project explores the tenderness and weight inside a phrase that can also be understood as “I don’t want to live without you” or “I’d die for you.”
