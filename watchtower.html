WATCHTOWER

2024

parsons core 3 studio

ux/ui, web app, html/css/js, civic design

visit site ↗

Watchtower is a community utility-tracking app designed for a downtown Manhattan neighborhood experiencing frequent power and water outages.

The project is built around three specific neighbors: a freelance mom, an elderly building super, and a punk rocker renovating his apartment. Different lives, same problem: nobody knows what is happening until it is already happening.

The app uses a color-coded real-time map, multi-channel reporting, language access, picture-based icons, and a resource-sharing layer. It is meant to work when the phone is dying, when the wifi is bad, and when the person using it does not have time for a perfect interface.
