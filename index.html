Resume About

Mi’Lah “mee-lah” Clark, also known as Artoflah, is a multidisciplinary graphic designer working between New Jersey and New York. She is currently studying Communication Design at Parsons School of Design with a minor in Fine Arts, creating work across book design, typography, print, web, UX/UI, motion, object-making, and whatever strange format the idea starts asking for. Her work is driven by color, tiny details, craft, and experiments that sometimes begin with no faith they’ll work. She likes building playful, thoughtful design systems that feel expressive, handmade, and a little unexpected.
